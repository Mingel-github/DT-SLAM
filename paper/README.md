# DT-SLAM paper workspace

This directory is the source of truth for planning the paper before the LaTeX
manuscript is created.

- `outline.md`: the paper's proposed reader-facing structure and the role of
  each section. It is not prose ready for submission.
- `evidence_matrix.md`: a claim-to-evidence ledger. Every numerical statement
  in a draft must trace to a reproducible result, its comparison condition, and
  its scope.

The final venue and paper title are intentionally undecided. Do not turn a
planned experiment or a single-run range check into a general claim.
